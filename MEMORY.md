## Session 10 device facts and measurements (Samsung Galaxy A32, 2026-10-10, development build, IL2CPP ARM64; §18 target devices and performance budget stay TBD: measured, not decided)

- **Samsung Galaxy A32 (SM-A325F, the test phone):** Android 13, Helio G80, 64-bit, 5.5 GB RAM, 1080×2400 at 60 Hz (a 90 Hz mode too), an 80 px camera cut-out (a side notch in landscape), no install blocker; a good worst case for §18 (about 4× slower than the desktop Editor). The HUD is laid out on a 1920×1080 reference scaled by height. The OPPO CPH1931 (2.7 GB RAM, Snapdragon 665 class, 720×1600 at 60 Hz) was plugged in first; its install was blocked by "Verify apps over USB" and the designer dropped it on 2026-10-10.
- **Hidden simulation per turn** (bot vs bot, 38 turns, `Logs` from the Device tab and `XRimDeviceLog.txt`): mean **58 ms**, 95% under **86 ms**, min 16 ms, **max 99.9 ms**. A full-length turn (360 steps, the 1.5 s hard cap) costs **83–86 ms**, about 0.23 ms per 240 Hz step; a short settled turn (106–160 steps) 30–50 ms. The Editor on the desktop measured mean 15.3 ms, max 43.6 ms (Session 09): this phone is about 4 times slower.
- **Frames:** 57 fps idle on the menu (frame mean 17.5 ms, 95% under 16.7), 55–57 fps during a bot match (mean 18 ms, 95% under 33 ms) with a **worst frame of about 66 ms**: the simulation runs on the main thread in one go when both plans lock, so every turn is one hitch of roughly 4 frames at a moment when the screen is still. Target 60 fps, vertical sync off, Swappy frame pacing on. **Pace:** bot vs bot about 1.9 s per turn on the phone (1 s watch pause + playback), the desktop pace: the simulation is not what makes a turn long.
- **Touch over adb (Android input, one finger):** a swipe down in the body strip set CROUCH, a stroke in the weapon zone drew the path preview and spent the ink (a stroke that starts far from the weapon tip spends it all on the D3 lead-in), Ready and the planning timeout both ran the turn (82 ms and 44 ms), no errors. The phone uses 3-button navigation, so Android's edge-back swipe could not steal a strip swipe; raw multi-touch injection (`sendevent`) is blocked by the phone's SELinux (permission denied), so two real fingers, edge palm rejection, a 90 Hz run and a gesture-navigation phone are for the designer's hands at PT4. **Not measured:** the weaker OPPO CPH1931 (2.7 GB RAM, Snapdragon 665 class). **Dropped by the designer 2026-10-10 ("we are never using it again hopefully"): it stays unmeasured, its install blocker ("Verify apps over USB") was never changed by Claude, and nothing more is planned for it.** Also not measured: thermal behaviour over a long session.
- **Proposal for the designer to decide (§18 performance budget; nothing is decided):** (1) the hidden simulation of one turn should take at most about **150 ms on the minimum supported phone, with a hard ceiling of 250 ms** (the Galaxy A32 needs 100 ms at worst, so it passes; a weaker phone is still to be measured); (2) the cost is one main-thread hitch between Ready and playback, at a still moment, so it is accepted while under the ceiling; if a weaker phone goes over, spread the simulation over a few frames (the 240 Hz step count never changes, only how many steps run per frame) rather than lowering the simulation rate; (3) 60 fps is the target during planning and playback (the game is light; 90 or 120 can be tried in the Display asset); (4) online play (Session 19, server authority) removes the phone's simulation cost, so the budget governs the offline modes; (5) the designer has to name a minimum phone class: the two test phones suggest "Android 8+, 64-bit, 3 GB RAM, Snapdragon 665 or Helio G80 class" as a floor to test against.
