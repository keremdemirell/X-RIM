## How to read this

- The bots are not people: the heuristic bot plays a readable plan from the public board, the random bot scribbles. Use the numbers to compare tunings and weapons with each other, not as a prediction of real matches.
- ± is the 95% margin of a share (a normal approximation): with 100 matches it is about ±10 points, so small differences are noise.
- "Looks strictly stronger" (the sidegrade rule, GDD §6, §16): a weapon whose win rate when carried is clearly above the others' average (beyond its 95% margin) and whose damage per swing is the highest of the weapons. "Looks weak": clearly below.
- Match time adds a person's planning, assumed 6 s a turn (Balance Targets asset, a placeholder), to the turns' time on screen with the feel layer.
